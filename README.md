# 🌅 Have a Good Day
 
A beautiful, single-page greeting web app that dynamically adapts its visuals, message, and animations based on the **time of day** and **season** — all in pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, no dependencies, no build step.
 
---
 
## ✨ Features
 
- **Time-aware greetings** — Automatically detects whether it's Morning, Forenoon, Afternoon, Evening, or Night and displays a matching greeting and color theme.
- **Season-aware themes** — Adapts to Summer, Monsoon, Autumn, Winter, or Spring with visual filters and bonus messages.
- **Animated particle system** — Unique canvas animations for each time and season:
  - 🌙 Night → Twinkling stars & falling shooting stars
  - 🌅 Morning → Sun rays & drifting clouds
  - ☀️ Forenoon → Floating leaves & clouds
  - 🌤️ Afternoon → Sun rays & clouds
  - 🌇 Evening → Glowing fireflies
  - ❄️ Winter → Falling snowflakes (layered on top)
  - 🌧️ Monsoon → Falling rain (layered on top)
- **Daily motivational messages** — 10 unique heartfelt messages per time period, plus season-specific ones. Refresh for a new one.
- **Live clock** — Displays the current time in the corner, updated every second.
- **Glassmorphism UI** — Frosted-glass card with smooth fade-in animation.
- **Fully responsive** — Works on mobile and desktop.

## 🎨 How It Works
 
### Time Periods
| Period | Hours | Theme |
|---|---|---|
| Morning | 5 AM – 9 AM | Purple → violet gradient |
| Forenoon | 10 AM – 11 AM | Teal → blue gradient |
| Afternoon | 12 PM – 4 PM | Gold → red gradient |
| Evening | 5 PM – 8 PM | Orange → golden gradient |
| Night | 9 PM – 4 AM | Deep navy gradient |
 
### Seasons
| Season | Months | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Summer | Apr – Jun | Vibrant, high saturation |
| Monsoon | Jul – Sep | Rain particles, darker filter |
| Autumn | Oct – Nov | Warm tones |
| Winter | Dec – Feb | Snow particles, muted tones |
| Spring | Mar | Fresh, bright |

- Pure **HTML5**
- **CSS3** — gradients, glassmorphism, keyframe animations
- **Vanilla JavaScript** — Canvas API for particle animations, Date API for time/season detection
